# 姑娘系列8：與病人基本英文對話

Created: 2023年12月6日 下午7:30
Updated: 2023年12月6日 下午7:30

**當病人來到診所時:**

- Good morning/ good afternoon
- What is your name? and What is your registered phone number?
- could you show me your ID card so i can check your record.
- What is your reservation time?
- Please have a seat and wait for your name to be called~ (坐低等等)

**當病人屬於新症時:**

- Are you the new patient?
- Please fill in the form~
- Please give me your ID card to help you check the information~
- **Please give me your ID card /passport for making the registration. (新症取證登記)**
- Are you on any medications? (你有沒有服藥中?)
- Are you allergic to anything? (你有沒有對任何野敏感?)
- The doctor will be with you shortly, **Have a seat please. (醫師好快見您, 坐低稍等)**

**當呼叫病人入醫師房時:**

- hello sir/miss , **you can enter room 1/ the doctor's room ~** (be gentle and soft^^)

**當呼叫病人取藥時:**

- **You can get the medicine now**~ (be gentle and soft^^)
- once / twice a day 、before/ after breakfast/lunch/dinner (服用方法)
- take 5 tablets a day (藥丸)
- **You need to take it __ times a day and each __ package before/ after meal**~
- **You have to take it with small amount of boiled/hot water. (about 150ml)**

**當收費時和幫病人預約時: (幫病人列印英文收據)**

**consultation fee 診金**

**herbal medicine 中藥**

**acupuncture 針灸**

**bone-setting treatment 正骨手法**

**cupping 拔罐 (pulling cupping 閃罐)**

- Hello, this is the charge(找贖). The total amount is $xxx. You can use cash or EPS.
- This is the change. (找贖)
- Do you need to book another appointment for further consultation ?
- Your next appointment is ____, is it right?
- Please feel free to contact us if you have any problems~ (給英文卡片)

**接受預約時:**

**i.  接聽電話的時候:**

- Hello, this is Eden Tcm Clinic, what can I help you?

**ii.** **預約時間的基本程序:**

- WHICH date you want to make the appointment?
- Which time is convenient? What time do you want to make an appointment? Do you prefer seeing the doctor in the morning section, or in the afternoon.
- Which doctor would you like to book an appointment for?
- Do you need acupuncture or any other treatment?
- Ok, please leave me your phone number
- Ok, I have made an appointment for you on 25/4 1:00 in Jordan/tw/central Clinic.

**iii.** **預約新症:**

- Ok, please leave me your full name and phone number~
- Is it your first time to come to the consultation?

**iv.** **約不到理想時間:**

- Sorry, that time is being booked/ ocuppied already. But there is another time at x:xx am/pm which is still available at the moment.
- Is it okay for you?
- (Yes) “Okay, please tell me your registered phone number”
- (No) “ Sorry, may I ask if you have any other days available ?”
- Sorry, Dr. XXX is all booked up on e.g. Monday. (全日滿約)
- Or "Sorry, if someone cancel the booking, I will call you as soon as possible, ok?"

**v.** **回覆未接來電和留言:**

- Hello, have you just called the Eden Tcm Clinic?
- Oh, sorry for missing your call, because we were closed at that time.

**特別情況或投訴**

- Hello, sorry, because there are lots of patient waiting here today, you may need to wait for a (bit long) period of time.

**與病人溝通技巧(治療室):**

- Hello, please come with me. (入治療房)
- Please change this suit, when u finished, press this button and I will come back and help you preparing for the treatment. (換衫)
- lie on the bed(treatment table) with facing up / down. (平訓/ 趴訓)
- turn on the heating lamp and you would feel a bit warm. (開燈)
- If u feel too hot /cold / uncomfortable, you may press the button anytime and I will come to help u immediately. (呼救鐘)
- take a rest, the doctor will come soon. (等醫師到)
- restart the heating lamp (MAYBE) Ask: is anything fine? (續燈)
- herbal treatment with the (far- infrared moisture ) heating pad (熱敷)
- remove/ withdraw the needles , (拔針)
- take it easy / just relax (安撫)
- The treatment is all done, you may change back ur own clothes and leave the room. (離開)
